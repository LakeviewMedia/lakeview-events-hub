OCC Shoebox Collection

Pack a Shoebox. Share the Gospel. Change a Life. 🎁❤️ Operation Christmas Child, a project of Samaritan’s Purse, delivers gift-filled shoeboxes to children in need around the world while sharing the Good News of Jesus Christ. Each shoebox is an opportunity to show God’s love in a tangible way and help bring joy, hope, and the message of the Gospel to a child.

Everyone can be part of the mission! Fill a shoebox with quality gifts, pray for the child who will receive it, and help us send the love of Jesus around the world—one shoebox at a time.

🎁 Pack a box.
🙏 Pray for a child.
🌎 Share God’s love.
❤️ Make an eternal impact.
